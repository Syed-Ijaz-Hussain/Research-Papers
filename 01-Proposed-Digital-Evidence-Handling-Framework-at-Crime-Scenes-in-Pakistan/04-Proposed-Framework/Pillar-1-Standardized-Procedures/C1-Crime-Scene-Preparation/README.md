## 1. Component Overview

**C1 — Crime-Scene Preparation, Authorization and Security** is the first component of Pillar 1 in the proposed framework, *Enhancing the Integrity and Security of Digital Evidence in Pakistan*.

This component focuses on the preparation, authorization, and security considerations that establish the conditions for digital evidence handling at a crime scene.

It addresses three central areas:

- **Preparation:** considerations for getting the crime scene ready for digital evidence handling.
- **Authorization:** ensuring that relevant activities are supported by appropriate authority.
- **Security:** maintaining control over the scene and access to areas where potential digital evidence is present.

The detailed requirements and procedures for these areas will be established through the project's verified research sources.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of C1 is to define a structured starting point for the crime-scene digital evidence handling process.

It is intended to help ensure that scene preparation, authorization, and security are considered before the subsequent component activities are carried out.

## 3. Scope

This component covers the preparation, authorization, and security aspects relevant to the crime-scene stage of the proposed framework.

The detailed scope will be refined through source-based research. C1 should not be used to describe laboratory examination, forensic analysis, or activities beyond the framework's defined crime-scene handling boundary.

The overall framework identifies the following roles:

- Digital Evidence Specialist (DES)
- Crime Scene Supervisor

- Access Control Officer

Their specific responsibilities within C1 must be established from the project's research and approved framework decisions. This page does not assign duties that have not yet been verified.
