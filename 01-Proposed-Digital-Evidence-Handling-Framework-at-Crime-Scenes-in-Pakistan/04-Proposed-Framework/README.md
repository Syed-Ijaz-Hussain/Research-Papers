## 1. Framework Purpose

This proposed framework is being developed as part of an academic research project to support a more standardized approach to digital evidence handling at crime scenes in Pakistan.

It organizes the crime-scene handling process into three connected pillars:

1. **Standardized Procedures** — how digital evidence is handled.
2. **Standardized Tools and Techniques** — which tools and methods are used to support handling activities.
3. **Chain of Custody** — how evidence handling, responsibility, and transfers are documented and tracked.

**Quality Assurance and Verification** is a cross-cutting supporting mechanism across all three pillars. It is not a fourth pillar.

## 2. Framework Architecture

| Framework element | Main question | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Pillar 1 — Standardized Procedures | How should evidence be handled? | Defines the structured crime-scene handling activities and their sequence. |
| Pillar 2 — Standardized Tools and Techniques | Which tools and techniques support the process? | Addresses the tools and methods used during relevant handling activities. |
| Pillar 3 — Chain of Custody | How is evidence handling and responsibility tracked? | Addresses documentation of handling, responsibility, and evidence transfers. |
| Cross-Cutting Quality Assurance and Verification | How is the process checked? | Supports consistency and verification across all three pillars. |

> **Important:** The framework has three pillars. Quality Assurance and Verification supports all three and must not be interpreted as a separate fourth pillar.

## 3. Pillar 1 — Standardized Procedures

Pillar 1 describes the procedural sequence for handling digital evidence at a crime scene. It currently contains six components:

| ID | Component | Main focus |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Crime-Scene Preparation, Authorization and Security | Preparing, authorizing, and securing the scene. |
| C2 | Digital Evidence Identification, Device Condition and Classification | Identifying potential digital evidence and recording device condition and classification. |
| C3 | Digital Evidence Prioritization, Volatility and Risk Assessment | Prioritizing evidence and considering volatility and relevant risks. |
| C4 | Initial Documentation and Scene Recording | Recording the scene and relevant initial observations. |
| C5 | Evidence Collection, Preservation and Contamination Prevention | Collecting and preserving evidence while reducing the risk of contamination or alteration. |
| C6 | Packaging, Labeling, Transportation and Scene Closure | Preparing evidence for secure transport and completing the scene-handling process. |

## 7. Framework Scope and Boundary

The proposed framework focuses on the **crime-scene handling stage** of digital evidence.

**Within the current scope**
- Crime-scene preparation and authorization.
- Identification, initial documentation, and prioritization of digital evidence.
- Collection and preservation activities at the scene.
- Packaging, labeling, and secure transportation.
- Documented, authorized handover of evidence.

**Outside the current scope**
- Laboratory examination and forensic analysis.
- Partition-level recovery and detailed data extraction.
- Interpretation of forensic findings.
- AI-based interpretation of evidence.
- Expert testimony and courtroom cross-examination.

The framework's process boundary ends at secure transportation and documented authorized handover. The excluded activities may be relevant to a wider digital forensics lifecycle, but they are not covered by this proposed framework.

## Research Integrity Note

This repository documents an academic research proposal. The framework is presented as a proposed research outcome; it must not be described as an officially approved national procedure, an adopted government policy, or an endorsed standard unless such approval or endorsement is independently established.

Detailed component content and framework requirements should be traceable to the project's verified research sources.
