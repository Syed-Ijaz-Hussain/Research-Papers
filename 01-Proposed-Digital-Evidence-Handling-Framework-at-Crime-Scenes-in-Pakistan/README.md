## 1. About the Research

This project proposes a framework for **standardized digital evidence handling at crime scenes in Pakistan**. It focuses on establishing a structured approach for identifying, documenting, collecting, preserving, packaging, transporting, and tracking digital evidence.

The research is informed by recognized digital-evidence standards and guidelines, while considering the Pakistani investigative and legal context.

> **Research direction:** A consistent and documented approach to crime-scene digital evidence handling, from initial scene preparation through secure transportation and authorized handover.

## 2. Framework at a Glance

The proposed framework is organized around **three core pillars**. **Quality Assurance and Verification** is a cross-cutting supporting mechanism across all three pillars—not a fourth pillar.

| Framework element | Main focus |
|---|---|
| **Pillar 1 — Standardized Procedures** | How digital evidence is handled at the crime scene |
| **Pillar 2 — Standardized Tools and Techniques** | Which tools and techniques support evidence-handling activities |
| **Pillar 3 — Chain of Custody** | How evidence handling, responsibility, and transfers are documented and tracked |
| **Cross-Cutting — Quality Assurance and Verification** | Supporting consistency and verification across all three pillars |

### Pillar 1: Standardized Procedures

Pillar 1 is structured into six components:

1. **C1 — Crime-Scene Preparation, Authorization and Security**
2. **C2 — Digital Evidence Identification, Device Condition and Classification**
3. **C3 — Digital Evidence Prioritization, Volatility and Risk Assessment**
4. **C4 — Initial Documentation and Scene Recording**
5. **C5 — Evidence Collection, Preservation and Contamination Prevention**
6. **C6 — Packaging, Labeling, Transportation and Scene Closure**

### Pillar 2: Standardized Tools and Techniques

This pillar addresses the tools and techniques relevant to standardized digital evidence handling. Its detailed documentation will be maintained in the dedicated section.

### Pillar 3: Chain of Custody

This pillar focuses on recording and tracking evidence handling, responsible persons, and transfers to support accountability and traceability.

### Cross-Cutting Quality Assurance and Verification

Quality Assurance and Verification supports all three pillars. It is represented as a cross-cutting mechanism rather than a separate framework pillar.

## 3. Research Scope

The framework focuses on crime-scene digital evidence handling and the documented handover of evidence.

**Included in scope**
- Crime-scene preparation, authorization, and security.
- Digital evidence identification and device-condition assessment.
- Prioritization, volatility, and risk assessment.
- Initial documentation and scene recording.
- Evidence collection, preservation, and contamination prevention.
- Packaging, labeling, transportation, and documented authorized handover.
- Chain-of-custody documentation and supporting verification.

**Outside the current scope**
- Laboratory examination and detailed forensic analysis.
- Partition-level recovery and interpretation of evidence.
- AI-based interpretation of digital evidence.
- Expert testimony and courtroom cross-examination.

## 4. Reference Standards and Guidance

The research uses the following reference documents to inform the framework:

- **ISO/IEC 27037:2012** — Guidelines for identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence.
- **NIJ (2008)** — Electronic crime scene investigation guidance.
- **ACPO (2012)** — Good practice guidance and principles for digital evidence.
- **Council of Europe C-PROC (2019)** — Relevant cybercrime and electronic evidence capacity-building reference material.

Refer to the [Reference Standards](03-Reference-Standards/) section for organized notes and supporting material. Always consult the original publications for authoritative wording and requirements.
